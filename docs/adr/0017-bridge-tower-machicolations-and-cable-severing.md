# Bridge tower machicolations, two-stage portcullis cable severing, and incendiary threat

Mission 2 (*Mříž*) required an overhaul because Ondřej could previously drop the portcullis on Turn 2 before the Swedish Occultist or Harriers posed an authentic tactical threat, leaving Father Plachý's upper gallery presence underutilized.

We resolved this by dividing the gate release into a cooperative two-stage operation: Ondřej (or another defender) must first unpin the windlass safety pawl on `y = 1` (`x = 11, y = 1, z = 14`), after which Father Plachý (or any blade-wielding defender) must sever the taut hemp hoisting cable on `y = 1` (`x = 12, y = 1, z = 14`) for 1 AP to slam the portcullis shut. To support Plachý's defensive role prior to descending to the rope, the western face on `y = 1` and `y = 2` now features dedicated Parléř-style machicolation embrasures providing directional `half` cover and High Ground plunging fire (+15% marksmanship) over the bridge deck. In turn, Swedish harriers at the threshold gain a 1-use incendiary pitch flask targeting the wooden windlass for 3 ongoing damage per turn (extinguishable for 1 AP), creating active counter-pressure while the Occultist advances toward the keystone.

## Considered Options

- **Automatic windlass crank without cable cutting**: Rejected because it reduced the victory condition to a passive wait timer on a single tile, bypassing Father Plachý's physical agency and leadership on the field.
- **Cutting the rope on the roof gallery (`y = 2`)**: Rejected because having the cable cut occur adjacent to the windlass drum on `y = 1` pulls Plachý down from the roof into the active combat volume of the tower, creating meaningful vertical movement and squad convergence.
- **Instant failure upon single wound during operation**: Rejected because resetting windlass progress from scratch on any graze caused frustrating deadlocks; wounds now stall the active turn's effort instead.
