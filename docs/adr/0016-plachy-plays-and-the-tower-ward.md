# Plachý plays, and the tower holds a ward

ADR-0014 left Father Jiří Plachý as the organizer of the Železná vigilie, and left demons and occultists undecided. The morning defense of the Old Town Bridge Tower needs a person the player is, and a reason the Swedish rush cannot simply walk into the Old Town.

The player plays Jiří Plachý. He still organizes the Vigil from the Clementinum, drawing unlike people and binding them by the blood-and-iron oath. On this morning he is on the field. If he falls, the mission is lost. He is not called Commander or Central.

Mission 2, **Mříž**, is that morning, 26 July 1648. No one from Mission 1 stands there: not Colloredo, not Jan Matěj, not Bratr Bartoloměj, not Václav z Michalovic, not Arnošt Odowalsky. With Plachý are two men the alarm caught on Křižovnické náměstí, not yet sworn: **Havel Řezáč**, a butcher, and **Ondřej Štěrba**, a thief of ropes and roofs. The oath is offered afterwards, to whoever still lives and accepts it.

The **Ochrana věže** (*Tower Ward*) is a holy hold. While it holds, demonic Swedish **harcovníci** cannot enter the tower or the square. They are on foot, without horses, and inhumanly fast. They may reach the **práh** (*threshold*) and shoot across it. They may not cross it. Plachý names the ward grace: the saints, the blessing of the gate, and the inscription *Signa te, signa…* as a call to make the sign of the cross. The **švédský okultista** treats the same ward as a formula he alone can unbind, by a rite on the keystone on the bridge side of the threshold. He is one man. He is not Odowalsky. His death ends the rite.

The **mříž** is iron. Once it drops, the bridge is cut from the tower even if the rite was still underway. Victory is the grate seated while the ward still holds. If the rite finishes first, the way is open and the mission is lost.

Rejected: the Mission 1 survivor leads the tower while Plachý stays inside the Clementinum; the harriers are ordinary musketeers and the ward is only a line in the briefing; Odowalsky is the occultist on the bridge.

The morning's working rules live in `GLOSSARY.md` under Mise 2: three floors, the three-turn rite, the windlass, the one kneeling, the harriers' tempo, and the oath afterwards.

This supersedes ADR-0014 on those two points only. Later Swedish forces are not infernal unless a later decision says so. Mission 1 and its survivor perks are unchanged, and those perks do not apply on this field.
