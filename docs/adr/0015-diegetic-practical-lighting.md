# Diegetic practical lighting

Night on Malá Strana reads as Caravaggio only if the warm light starts at something the player can see burning. A handful of point lights hung in empty space, plus a second muzzle light on every shot, made the palace and the garden the same amber wash and lit the smoke twice.

Practical light is capped. Four candelabra in Colloredo's rooms, three torch-class slots shared with the Čertovka lantern, and two matchcord slots. Blasts stay at two and muzzles at one (ADR-0011). None of the practicals cast shadows. A shrouded matchcord takes a slot ahead of a nearer visible coal, because that glow is the contact. Wheellocks, including the marshal's pistol, stay dark until they fire. A misfire sparks at the pan. A shot lights the barrel tip.

Rain gutters flames that stand in the open, including courtyard cressets whose pillar cell sits on the arcade lip. Candles under the palace roof do not gutter. Fog shortens the radius and hides the shafts. Shafts remain only over the courtyard well and the garden. A scorched fixture tile goes out. Smoke, dust, and rain read this same census: smoke keeps its amber core only for a muzzle or a blast, dust stays mineral except inside that flash, and a rain streak glints only within a short reach of an outdoor flame. The smoke tactics in ADR-0011 are unchanged.

A light on every musketeer was rejected. WebGL still folds every point light into every lit material, and the movement tiles have to stay readable.
